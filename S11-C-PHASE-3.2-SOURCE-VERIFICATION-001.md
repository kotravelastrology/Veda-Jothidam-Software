## Chapter 36 — Miscellaneous/Foundational Yogas (3 Primary)

### 1. **Gaja Kesari Yoga** (BPHS 36.1-2)

**Formation Rule**: 
- Jupiter in an angular house (1, 4, 7, 10) from the Moon
- No other condition required

**Effects**: 
- Native becomes wise, virtuous, and elephant-like in strength/valor
- Elephant's might (gaja) + lion's courage (kesari)
- Prosperity, good fortune, respect
- Leadership and commanding presence
- Long life and health

**BPHS Citation**: "When Jupiter occupies an angle from the Moon, Gaja Kesari Yoga is formed." (Ch. 36, v. 1-2)

**Implementation Notes**:
- Simple house check relative to Moon
- Angular = 1st, 4th, 7th, 10th from Moon

---

### 2. **Shubha Yoga** (BPHS 36.3-4)

**Formation Rule**: 
- Any benefic planet (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) in an angle (1, 4, 7, 10) from the Ascendant
- Unafflicted by malefics

**Effects**: 
- Native becomes auspicious (shubha = auspicious)
- Good fortune, favorable circumstances
- Health and well-being
- Success in undertakings

**BPHS Citation**: "When benefics occupy angles from the Ascendant, Shubha Yoga is formed." (Ch. 36, v. 3-4)

**Implementation Notes**:
- Angular = 1, 4, 7, 10
- Check any benefic in these positions

---

### 3. **Ashubha Yoga** (BPHS 36.5-6)

**Formation Rule**: 
- Any malefic planet (Mars, Saturn, Sun) in an angle (1, 4, 7, 10) from the Ascendant
- Unaspected by benefics

**Effects**: 
- Native becomes inauspicious (ashubha = inauspicious)
- Bad luck, unfavorable circumstances
- Health problems, obstacles
- Difficulties in undertakings

**BPHS Citation**: "When malefics occupy angles from the Ascendant, Ashubha Yoga is formed." (Ch. 36, v. 5-6)

**Implementation Notes**:
- Opposite of Shubha Yoga
- Check any malefic in angular positions
