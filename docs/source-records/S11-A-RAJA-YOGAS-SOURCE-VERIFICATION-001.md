## Yoga-by-Yoga Documentation

### Core Raja Yogas (Verses 6-12) — Foundational Combinations

#### 1. **Maha Raja Yoga** (BPHS 39.6-7)
- **Formation Rule**: 
  - Ascendant lord and 5th lord exchange signs (swap houses/signs), OR
  - Atmakaraka and Putra Karaka (5th lord) in ascendant/5th/exaltation/own sign/own Navamsha, aspected by benefice
- **Effects**: Famous, happy, very elevated status, royal elevation
- **Implementation Notes**: 
  - Check for sign exchange (exact mutual exchange)
  - OR check Atmakaraka + Putra Karaka (need Karakamsa if using 5th lord definition)
  - Simplified version: Ascendant lord + 5th lord in mutual angles aspected by benefice

#### 2. **Raja Yoga (Atmakaraka-based)** (BPHS 39.8)
- **Formation Rule**: Ascendant lord and Atmakaraka conjunct OR aspected in ascendant, 5th, 7th; aspected by benefice
- **Effects**: Raja yoga conferring royal status
- **Implementation Notes**: Requires Atmakaraka calculation

#### 3. **Raja Yoga (2nd-4th-5th Placement)** (BPHS 39.9-10)
- **Formation Rule**: 
  - Benefices in 2nd, 4th, 5th from Ascendant lord or Atmakaraka
  - Malefics in 3rd, 6th (not 8th/12th)
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Count benefices/malefics from Ascendant lord's position

#### 4. **Venus Royal Association Yoga** (BPHS 39.11)
- **Formation Rule**: Venus in Karakamsa OR 5th from Karakamsa OR ascendant OR Arudha ascendant, aspected by/conjunct Jupiter or Moon
- **Effects**: Related to royal circles, patronage
- **Implementation Notes**: Karakamsa-dependent; simplify to Venus in angle with Jupiter/Moon aspect

#### 5. **Single Planet Aspect Yoga** (BPHS 39.12)
- **Formation Rule**: Single planet (strong/exalted) aspects natal Ascendant OR Hora Lagna OR Ghatika Lagna
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Requires divisional chart calculations (Hora, Ghatika Lagna)

### Divisional & Multi-Ascendant Yogas (Verses 13-27)

#### 6. **Shad Varga Raja Yoga** (BPHS 39.13-14)
- **Formation Rule**: Same planet occupies/aspects Ascendant in ALL 6 divisions (Rashi, Hora, Drekkana, Trimsamsa, Navamsha, Dvadashamsa)
- **Effects**: Full Raja yoga if full aspect; medium if half; negligible if quarter
- **Implementation Notes**: Requires all 6 divisional charts; highly specialized

#### 7. **Three Ascendants Raja Yoga** (BPHS 39.15)
- **Formation Rule**: 
  - 3 Ascendants (natal Ascendant, Hora Lagna, Ghatika Lagna) occupied by exalted/own sign planets, OR
  - Natal, Drekkana, Navamsha Ascendants have exalted planets
- **Effects**: Raja yoga formed
- **Implementation Notes**: Requires Hora Lagna, Ghatika Lagna calculations

#### 8. **Arudha Lagna Raja Yoga** (BPHS 39.16)
- **Formation Rule**: Moon and benefice in Arudha Lagna + Jupiter in 2nd from natal Ascendant; both places aspected by exalted/own sign planets
- **Effects**: Strong Raja yoga
- **Implementation Notes**: Arudha Lagna dependent

#### 9. **Royal Placement Yoga** (BPHS 39.17)
- **Formation Rule**: Ascendant, 2nd, 4th occupied by benefices; malefic in 3rd
- **Effects**: Native becomes king or equal to king
- **Implementation Notes**: Simple positional check; no dependence on other calculations

#### 10. **2nd House Exaltation Yoga** (BPHS 39.18)
- **Formation Rule**: Moon, Jupiter, Venus, or Mercury exalted in 2nd house
- **Effects**: Native becomes wealthy
- **Implementation Notes**: Check exaltation status in 2nd

#### 11. **Debilitation Raja Yoga (1)** (BPHS 39.19)
- **Formation Rule**: 6th, 8th, 3rd occupied by debilitated planets; Ascendant lord exalted/own house, aspects Ascendant
- **Effects**: Raja yoga from overcoming afflictions
- **Implementation Notes**: Inverse logic — debilitation converted to strength

#### 12. **Debilitation Raja Yoga (2)** (BPHS 39.20)
- **Formation Rule**: 6th, 8th, 12th lords in debilitation/inimical signs/combustion; Ascendant lord in own/exaltation aspects Ascendant
- **Effects**: Raja yoga from affliction reversal
- **Implementation Notes**: Similar to #11, more specific conditions

#### 13. **10th Lord Raja Yoga** (BPHS 39.21)
- **Formation Rule**: 10th lord in own house or exaltation aspects Ascendant; benefices in angles
- **Effects**: Raja yoga formed
- **Implementation Notes**: Check Karma Lagna (10th house) lord

#### 14. **Atmakaraka Wealth Yoga** (BPHS 39.22)
- **Formation Rule**: Atmakaraka in benefice's Rashi (sign) or Navamsha
- **Effects**: Native becomes wealthy
- **Implementation Notes**: Requires Atmakaraka; check if in friendly sign

#### 15. **Karakamsa Benefic Yoga** (BPHS 39.22)
- **Formation Rule**: Benefices in angles (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) from Karakamsa Lagna
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Karakamsa-dependent

#### 16. **Arudha Pada - Dara Pada Yoga** (BPHS 39.23)
- **Formation Rule**: Arudha Lagna and Darapada (spouse Arudha) in mutual angles OR mutual 3rd/11th OR mutual trines
- **Effects**: Native becomes king without doubt
- **Implementation Notes**: Arudha Lagna + Darapada relationship checks

#### 17. **Three Ascendants Aspect Yoga** (BPHS 39.24)
- **Formation Rule**: 2 or all 3 of Bhava Lagna, Hora Lagna, Ghatika Lagna in aspect to exalted planets
- **Effects**: Raja yoga
- **Implementation Notes**: Divisional Lagna aspect checks

#### 18. **Ascendant Divisional Yoga** (BPHS 39.25)
- **Formation Rule**: 3 Ascendants (natal, decanates, Navamsha) OR combinations aspected by planet
- **Effects**: Raja yoga
- **Implementation Notes**: Multiple divisional layers

#### 19. **Arudha Pada Exaltation Yoga** (BPHS 39.26-27)
- **Formation Rule**: Arudha Pada occupied by exalted planet (Moon preferred) OR Jupiter/Venus without malefic Argala
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Arudha Lagna + exaltation check + Argala analysis

#### 20. **Arudha Pada Moon-Jupiter Yoga** (BPHS 39.27)
- **Formation Rule**: Arudha Pada is benefice sign with Moon + Jupiter in 2nd house
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Arudha sign compatibility + Jupiter placement

#### 21. **Debilitated Single Lord Yoga** (BPHS 39.28)
- **Formation Rule**: ONE among 6th, 8th, 12th lords debilitated AND aspects Ascendant
- **Effects**: Raja yoga (affliction becomes strength)
- **Implementation Notes**: Specific condition — only one 6/8/12 lord debilitated

### Ascendant-Lord & Angular-Trinal Combinations (Verses 29-38)

#### 22. **Angular Lord Aspect Yoga** (BPHS 39.29-31)
- **Formation Rule**: 
  - Planet ruling 4th, 10th, 2nd, 11th aspects Ascendant, OR
  - Venus aspects 11th from Arudha Lagna, OR
  - Arudha Lagna occupied by benefice
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Multiple conditions; any one satisfies

#### 23. **Debilitated Placement Raja Yoga (3)** (BPHS 39.29-31)
- **Formation Rule**: Debilitated planet in 3rd/11th lends aspect to Ascendant
- **Effects**: Similar Raja yoga effects (affliction reversal)
- **Implementation Notes**: 3rd/11th house (non-8th/12th) allows debilitation reversal

#### 24. **Planetary Aspects Raja Yoga** (BPHS 39.32)
- **Formation Rule**: Raja yogas based on planets with different dignities and aspects/conjunctions
- **Effects**: Royal status
- **Implementation Notes**: Umbrella category; documented in text as variations

#### 25. **5th and 9th Lord Yoga** (BPHS 39.33-34)
- **Formation Rule**: 
  - 9th lord (like minister) and 5th lord mutually aspect OR
  - Conjunct in any house OR
  - In 7th places (7th and 1st from a reference)
- **Effects**: Native obtains kingdom; royal scion becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Multiple conditions; strong yoga for kingship

#### 26. **Angular-Trinal Lord Exchange Yoga** (BPHS 39.35)
- **Formation Rule**: 4th lord in 10th, 10th lord in 4th; both aspect 5th and 9th lords
- **Effects**: Native attains kingdom
- **Implementation Notes**: Requires all 4 lords' positions

#### 27. **Multiple Lord Conjunction Yoga** (BPHS 39.36)
- **Formation Rule**: 5th, 10th, 4th, Ascendant lords join in 9th house
- **Effects**: Native becomes ruler with fame spreading in four directions
- **Implementation Notes**: 4 lords conjunct in single house — rare, powerful

#### 28. **Angular-Trinal Conjunction Yoga (1)** (BPHS 39.37)
- **Formation Rule**: 4th lord joins 5th or 9th lord
- **Effects**: Native obtains kingdom
- **Implementation Notes**: 4th lord with either 5th or 9th lord

#### 29. **Angular-Trinal Conjunction Yoga (2)** (BPHS 39.37)
- **Formation Rule**: 10th lord joins 5th or 9th lord
- **Effects**: Native obtains kingdom
- **Implementation Notes**: 10th lord with either 5th or 9th lord

#### 30. **5th Lord Placement Yoga** (BPHS 39.38)
- **Formation Rule**: 5th lord in Ascendant, 4th, or 10th + with 9th lord or Ascendant lord
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: 5th in angle + conjunction with 9th/Asc lord

#### 31. **Jupiter 9th House Yoga** (BPHS 39.39)
- **Formation Rule**: Jupiter in own sign identical with 9th house + with Venus or 5th lord
- **Effects**: Native obtains kinghood
- **Implementation Notes**: Jupiter's own sign in 9th + specific planetary company

### Timing-Based & Multiple Exaltation Yogas (Verses 40-48)

#### 32. **Dinardha Nisardha Yoga** (BPHS 39.40)
- **Formation Rule**: Birth within 2.5 ghatis (60 minutes) from mid-day (Dinardha) or mid-night (Nisardha)
- **Effects**: Native becomes king or equal to king
- **Implementation Notes**: Time of birth check; requires precise birth time

#### 33. **Koteeswara Yoga** (BPHS 39.40, ref. Uttara Kalamrita)
- **Formation Rule**: Birth within 2 ghatikas of Dinardha/Nisardha + exalted planet in 2nd aspecting another exalted
- **Effects**: King who conquers enemies, observes religious rules, learned
- **Implementation Notes**: Timing + dual exaltation check

#### 34. **Lakshad Heewara Yoga** (BPHS 39.40, ref. Uttara Kalamrita)
- **Formation Rule**: Birth within 2 ghatikas (Dinardha/Nisardha) + aspecting planet in own house (not exalted)
- **Effects**: Relatively less riches (variant of Dinardha yoga)
- **Implementation Notes**: Timing + own-sign placement (not exalted)

#### 35. **Moon-Venus Mutual Yoga** (BPHS 39.41)
- **Formation Rule**: Moon and Venus in mutual 3rd and 11th with aspect
- **Effects**: Raja yoga obtained
- **Implementation Notes**: Moon-Venus angular relationship (3/11 or mutual aspect)

#### 36. **Moon-Venus Aspect Yoga** (BPHS 39.41)
- **Formation Rule**: Moon and Venus mutually aspect (anywhere in chart)
- **Effects**: Raja yoga obtained
- **Implementation Notes**: Simple mutual aspect check

#### 37. **Moon Vargothamsa Yoga** (BPHS 39.42)
- **Formation Rule**: Moon endowed with strength + in Vargothamsa + aspected by 4+ planets
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Requires Moon strength assessment + Vargothamsa check + multi-planet aspect count

#### 38. **Ascendant Uttamamsa Yoga** (BPHS 39.43)
- **Formation Rule**: Ascendant in Uttamamsa (highest divisional dignity) + aspected by 4+ planets (Moon not included)
- **Effects**: Native becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Divisional check + aspect count

#### 39. **Exalted Planets Yoga (1-3 planets)** (BPHS 39.44)
- **Formation Rule**: 1, 2, or 3 planets in exaltation in natal chart
- **Effects**: Royal scion becomes king; another equal to king or wealthy
- **Implementation Notes**: Count exalted planets; variable effects by count

#### 40. **Exalted Planets Yoga (4-5 planets)** (BPHS 39.45)
- **Formation Rule**: 4-5 planets in exaltation or Moola-Trikona signs
- **Effects**: Even person of base-birth becomes king
- **Implementation Notes**: Requires Moola-Trikona data (first 3°20' of signs for most planets)

#### 41. **Six Exalted Planets Yoga** (BPHS 39.46)
- **Formation Rule**: 6 planets in exaltation
- **Effects**: Native becomes emperor, enjoys various royal paraphernalia
- **Implementation Notes**: Extremely rare condition; powerful yoga

#### 42. **Jupiter-Venus-Mercury Exaltation Yoga** (BPHS 39.47)
- **Formation Rule**: One among Jupiter, Venus, Mercury in exaltation + benefice in angle
- **Effects**: Native becomes king or equal to king
- **Implementation Notes**: Only ONE of these three exalted + benefice in angle

#### 43. **Benefics and Malefics Placement Yoga** (BPHS 39.48)
- **Formation Rule**: All benefices in angles (1, 4, 7, 10) + malefics in 3rd, 6th, 11th
- **Effects**: Person of mean descent ascends throne
- **Implementation Notes**: Strict positional requirements; benefice constraint in angles

- **Formation Rule**: Malefics in 6th, 8th, 12th; 6th/8th/12th lords weak/debilitated; these lords aspect Ascendant
- **Effects**: Raja yoga from affliction turning to strength
- **Implementation Notes**: Already covered in Debilitation Raja Yogas; included for completeness

- **Formation Rule**: Two planets exchange signs (Parivartana/mutual sign exchange)
- **Effects**: Strengthens house linkage; often becomes Raja yoga in angles/trines
- **Implementation Notes**: General principle used in multiple specific yogas

3. **Phase 1C**: Advanced divisional yogas as time permits (specialized use cases)
