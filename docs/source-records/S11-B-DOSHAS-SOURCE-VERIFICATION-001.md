### 1. **Pitra Dosha** (Father's Curse)
**BPHS Chapter**: 83 (specific verse TBD via Santhanam PDF)  
**Formation Rule**: 
- Sun or 9th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th house, OR
- Sun debilitated or combust, OR
- 9th house/9th lord afflicted by malefics
- Additional: Weak or absent father relationship indicators

**Effects**: 
- Obstacles to paternal inheritance
- Lack of father's blessing/support
- Career stagnation despite effort
- Health issues (especially to male children)
- Ancestral curses manifest

**Remedies** (from BPHS context):
- Worship Sun (father's significator)
- Appease ancestors (Pitri Puja)
- Perform Pada Pooja
- Charity to elderly men
- Respect for elders

**Implementation Notes**: 
- Check Sun position in 6/8/12
- Check 9th lord position in 6/8/12
- Check Sun's exaltation/debilitation status
- Check malefic aspects to 9th

### 3. **Sarpa Dosha** (Serpent's Curse)
**BPHS Chapter**: 83  
**Formation Rule**: 
- Rahu or Ketu in 5th, 8th, or 12th house, OR
- Rahu/Ketu conjunct Sun/Mars/Saturn in these houses, OR
- 5th lord with Rahu/Ketu, OR
- Past-life karma connections (snake harm)

**Effects**: 
- Sudden unexpected problems
- Miscarriage or child health issues
- Obstacles in family expansion
- Skin diseases / allergic reactions
- Accidents and sudden losses
- Chronic health problems

**Remedies**: 
- Worship Shiva (controller of snakes)
- Perform Sarpa Dosha Nivaran Puja
- Feed snakes (milk/cream - metaphorically)
- Navagraha Shanti (9-planet appeasement)
- Avoid snake harm in actions

**Implementation Notes**: 
- Rahu/Ketu position check (but not in standard PLANETS array)
- 5th lord position in 6/8/12
- Conjunction checks with malefics in sensitive houses
- Note: Simplified without Rahu/Ketu if not in chart data

**Severity**: High (sudden/unexpected)
