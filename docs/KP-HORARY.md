# KP horary (`/kp-horary`)

Source: K.S. Krishnamurti, *Horary Astrology: Krishnamurti Padhdhati, Advanced Stellar System* (Reader VI; corpus C34, second scan C68). Printed page = PDF - 19 up to about PDF 190,
then PDF - 21 (read from the page number printed on each page). Engine `src/report/kpHorary.js`, questions `src/report/kpHoraryTopics.js`, page `app/kp-horary`, test `test-kp-horary.js`.

## What the book says (read)
- The querist gives a number 1-249; "every number refers to a particular sign, star and sub as the Ascendant. One has to take only the commencing position of the sub as Ascendant" (printed p.88).
- "We must take only the time of judgment" (p.87), not the time of the urge, the posting or the receipt; standard time is used as it is (p.88): the number fixes the ascendant, so no local mean time or
  sidereal time is needed for it. "Having this as Ascendant, one is to calculate the other 11 cusps for the latitude of the place of judgment" (p.88). Implemented by finding the instant at which that
  ascendant rises at the place and taking the Placidus cusps of that instant (Krishnamurti ayanamsha); the planets are those of the time of judgment.
- The table of the 249 subs (printed pp.89-106): sign lord, star lord, sub lord, from and to. Computed here (243 subs of the 27 stars, three of them cut at a sign boundary = 249) and compared with the book:
  rows 10-41 on the page image, and the text layer for the rest (207 of 243 numbers equal; the others are OCR slips such as 25.63.20 for 25.53.20).
- Ruling planets (printed p.123): the day lord, the lord of the star and of the sign the Moon is in, the lord of the star and the sign of the ascendant; Rahu and Kethu stand for the lord of the sign they
  are in and are counted when that lord is ruling; "those deposited in the constellation of a retrograde planet should be rejected" (marked, not removed).
- Questions and their houses, where the text names them: 74 of the book's about 110 (`kpHoraryTopics.js` for the first 17, `kpHoraryMore.js` for the rest), each with the printed page the page itself shows and my Tamil rendering of the reason the book gives.
  Where the text states a condition, it is kept as a rule: the sub-lord of a cusp (or the lord of the star it stands in) signifies all of, or one of, a list of houses (the book's "and" read as both, "or" as either), with the direct-motion and badhaka/maraka
  conditions where the text has them: love affairs (5th cusp), invalid child, partner in business (8th), actor, treasure, dowry, buying him, lottery (11th), going abroad (12th), partnership (7th), suicide and murder (8th and 1st with the badhaka or maraka),
  reinstatement (10th), income-tax prosecution (10th), the good times to buy, sell, enter service, sign an agreement, engage a servant (2nd, 12th, 6th, 3rd and 7th, 1st) and promotion on probation (6th). The rules say that the condition holds; the page gives no answer.

## What the page shows
The sub of the number, the twelve cusps with sign, star and sub lords, the planets of the time of judgment (retrograde marked), the ruling planets, and for a chosen question: the sub-lord of each of its cusps and the
houses it signifies, every planet that signifies the question's houses (and which of 6, 8, 12 it also signifies), and which ruling planets are among them. No answer is given.

## Not built
The questions whose houses are worked through derived houses (those counted from a relative's lagna, such as a third son's house, the father's whereabouts, a will from an uncle, relatives' luck, adoption, change of job, politician, seniority, cure, which gem) are not entered; the ruling-planet examples of printed pp.123-142 (friend's arrival, parcels, the train, birth time) and the long-life and eclipsed-planet chapters are not either. The section on the twelve houses (pp.116-122), the time-of-event working from the ruling planets and the
ascendant's movement, and the judgement lists of each section are not. The author's other readers added to the corpus on 2026-10-26 (C64 Reader I, C65 Reader II, C66-C68 second scans of III, V, VI, C69 KP-Ezine, C70
Astro Secrets IV) are registered but not yet read for rules; Reader V (transit) is next.

## Reader VI printed pp.107-121: signs, "how to judge" and the twelve houses (2026-10-27)

`src/report/kpJudgeTables.js` holds, as printed with a Tamil rendering, the classification of the signs (24 classes), five sign-keyed paragraph sets (appearance and character from the ascendant sub-lord's star lord, health and disease from the 6th cusp, finance and fortune from the 2nd, romance from the 5th and, for the partner, the 7th) and what the twelve houses signify. `src/report/kpJudge.js` picks, for each cusp, the paragraph of the sign that the star lord of the sub-lord occupies; a node as star lord is reported, not read. Shown in the KP marriage section of the report builder. Descriptions only. Pinned by `test-kp-judge.js`.

## Ruling planets: the positions that rise (Reader VI printed pp.123-142, 2026-10-27)

For an event within minutes or hours the book takes no dasa: it finds the zodiac position whose sign, star, sub (and sub-sub) lords are all ruling planets and says when it rises (pp.126, 129-130). `src/report/kpRulingTime.js` lists every such position in the next hours (default 6) with the instants the ascendant enters and leaves it; /kp-horary shows them. The significator conditions of the worked examples are not applied there. Pinned by `test-kp-ruling-time.js` against two printed examples: the Madras messenger (Venus/Sun/Saturn/Mercury at 3°30' Taurus: 15:58:52-16:00:02, book "4 P.M.") and the Matale trunk call (Venus/Jupiter/Sun/Venus at 29°20' Libra: 9:39:49-9:40:17, book 9:41 from its sidereal-time table, about a minute later; recorded). The "friend's arrival" example (p.127) names no place, so it is not reproduced.

## Ruling planets beyond the ascendant: Moon, Sun, Jupiter and the birth time (Reader VI printed pp.131-141, 2026-10-27)

/kp-ruling (`src/report/kpRulingTime.js`, `app/kp-ruling/`): the ruling planets of a moment of judgment, then the positions they rule and when the thing that moves reaches them. Printed p.131 (N.B.): minutes or hours, the ascendant; days within a month, the Moon; beyond a month, the Sun; years, Jupiter. Moon is listed to sub-sub level, Sun and Jupiter to sub level (a sub-sub is narrower than their steps). The checkbox drops planets standing in the star of a retrograde planet (p.123; off by default because the book's own trunk-call example keeps them). Birth time (pp.138-141): the positions that rose within the window the birth is known to lie in. Reproduced from the book: 16-10-1919 (Mercury/Moon/Rahu/Saturn, 5:23:21-5:24:42, book 5:23:50) and 13-11-1948 (Mercury/Moon/Sun/Mercury, 3:36:20-3:36:44, book 3:36:45). Recorded difference: the receipt-of-drugs example (p.133-134) has the Moon at 11°27' Virgo at 1:15 P.M. on 13-9-1969 where the ephemeris here has 14°31', so the Moon reaches the printed Rahu-Jupiter-Venus point at 11:06:48 on 15-9-1969, not at the printed 2:39 P.M. The 1967 politician, the 1913 date and the Delhi examples (pp.141-143) name a person or give no full data and are not reproduced.

## Reader VI printed pp.144-153: strength, the retrograde star and the eclipsed planet (2026-10-27)

`src/report/kpStrength.js`, shown with the sign paragraphs in the KP report section. K.P. grades no planet by exaltation or retrogression in a natal chart; it reads (1) the retrograde star (pp.146-147): the significators of 2/7/11 (marriage), 1/6/10 (divorce), 3/12 (imprisonment), 6/11 (victory) that stand in the constellation of a retrograde planet are listed as blocked, the rest as free, and the 7th cusp sub-lord rule of p.146 is stated; nodes are never retrograde; (2) the twelve paragraphs of printed pp.151-152 for the sub-lord of each cusp standing in the star of an eclipsed planet. The book gives no orb for "eclipsed", so the distance of the star lord from the Sun is shown and no planet is declared eclipsed. Pages 154-157 (how long one will live, number 246 example) are about the length of life and are not built. Pinned by `test-kp-strength.js`.

## Reader V lucky timings (printed pp.219-234, 2026-10-27)

/kp-lucky-time (`src/report/kpLuckyTime.js`, `app/kp-lucky-time/`): 25 matters with the houses the book names to take and to avoid (treasure, sick child, tender, film, lottery ticket, overdraft, bank account, court filing, strike notice, mantra, music, donation, agreement, love meeting, parents' consent, entering a house, taking a vehicle, scholarship letter, passport, college admission, airport, conceiving). A planet qualifies when it signifies at least one house to take and none to avoid (the four levels of kpHouseSignificators.js). Reported for a chart: the dasa-bhukti-anthra periods whose three lords all qualify, the Moon's windows in the star and sub of qualifying planets on a day (rulingTransitMoments with `requireSign: false`), and the ascendant's sign-star-sub windows that day, flagged when inside a Moon window. Conditions only; the book itself says the moment helps only where the horoscope allows (pp.223, 229). The cricket entry of p.231 names no houses and is not built; the "trapped taking a bribe" example of p.226 is an adverse case and is not built; cards and horse racing (pp.195-218) apply the same method to a gambler's own chart and are not separate entries. Pinned by `test-kp-lucky-time.js`.

## Reader II printed pp.97-116: the thirteen groups of the twelve signs (2026-10-27)

`src/report/readerIISigns.js` (table and chart reading) and `src/report/readerIISignTables.js` (the book's text, English as printed with a Tamil summary, 27 sections, pp.97-116). The table of groups is checked against every list the book prints (`test-reader-ii-signs.js`). Sagittarius is split at 15 degrees: first half human and bicorporal, second half bestial. Libra is humane to some authors only and is not listed as human. A chart reading gives, for each house cusp, the group of its sign and the book's sections for those groups, and counts the seven classical planets by element, quality and polarity. The book's "majority of planets" is read as four or more of the seven; this is our reading, stated in the output. Shown in the KP report section. Pages 117-271 (the sign-by-sign meanings) are not built.

## Reader II printed pp.117-271: what each sign signifies (2026-10-27, in progress)

`src/report/readerIISignDescriptions.js`, generated by `r2_signs.py` (slices the book's text by the TOC's sign pages and its topic labels) and `r2_gen_signs.py` (joins each topic's Tamil summary from `r2_ta_<sign>.py`). Built so far: Aries (pp.117-124, sixteen topics: physical features, mind, characteristics, health, finance, romance, domestic environment, profession, places, direction, products, countries and cities, handwriting, lucky day, number and stone) Taurus (pp.124-138, twenty topics, including weakness, disease, children, colour, cities, animate and inanimate objects and the note on correcting the sign) and Gemini (pp.138-150, eighteen topics, including places, handwriting and the sign's own lucky items). Each topic carries its printed page. Pinned by `test-reader-ii-sign-descriptions.js`, `test-reader-ii-sign-descriptions-taurus.js` and `test-reader-ii-sign-descriptions-gemini.js`. The other nine signs, the twelve houses (pp.272-344) and the planets (pp.345-488) are not yet built. Not yet shown in the KP report.
