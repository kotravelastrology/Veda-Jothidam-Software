# The muhurta star — Latta, Vainashika, the 88th and 108th pada (2026-10-10)

A muhurta's star is the one the Moon stands in. For one person and a period
(7–90 days, from their local midnight), the period is cut wherever the Moon or
a kicking planet enters a new pada (3°20′), the Sun changes degree, the tithi
or yoga changes, or a planet enters or leaves Abhijit; in each piece every
check below is constant, and the page marks the piece clear or rejected under
the chosen readings.

## Latta — for anyone

The transit latta's counts (Phaladeepika XXVI.42–44, `/latta`), applied to the
muhurta star: Muhurta Chintamani VI.56 — Mercury 7th, Rahu 9th, the full Moon
22nd, Venus 5th backward; the Sun 12th, Saturn 8th, Jupiter 6th, Mars 3rd
forward (the planet's own star is the 1st; Abhijit not counted).

Eight books, by words on the subject: Shridhar 893, Muhurta Chintamani (G.C.
Sharma) 402, Rangacharya 250, Joshi 223, Wilhelm 192, Kalyanraman 153, Agarwal
73, Vashistha 58. They agree on the counts and differ on three things — all
computed, Shridhar's choice the default:

| Point | Readings |
|---|---|
| Rahu's direction | **backward** — MC VI.56 verse, Shridhar (table 8.7: the 20th counted forward), Kalyanraman, Joshi, Vashistha, Wilhelm · forward — MC commentary, Agarwal, Rangacharya's rule (but his reverse table and Swati example are backward) |
| Which quarter | **whole star** — MC VI.56; Shridhar: the pada "only in urgency" · the quarter equal to the kicking planet's — MC VI.64 "नेष्टोऽङ्घ्रिः खेटपत्समः", Wilhelm, Rangacharya (Joshi, Agarwal: "more malefic") · Jyotirvidabharana (via Shridhar): forward kicks the 1st quarter, backward the 4th |
| The Moon | her kick (22nd backward) cannot land on her own star — not computed; Shridhar guesses the lagna's star |

Ketu is in none of the muhurta lists. The effects (Shridhar's table 8.7, MC,
Joshi, Wilhelm, Kalyanraman) are shown as the books' words, collapsed.

## For the person

By words: Shridhar 181, Kalyanraman 148, Wilhelm 121, Kalaprakasika 85, MC
(Govinda's commentary) 59.

- **Birth star and pada** — Kalyanraman vol.1 p.126; Wilhelm p.93 (not after midday).
- **88th pada** from the birth pada — every book. Within it, the stretches when
  the lagna's lord and the 10th lord are friends are listed (Kalaprakasika p.190,
  Wilhelm p.94, Shridhar p.227: the evil "will vanish"); our reading: BPHS natural
  friendship both ways, or one lord; the lagna for the birth place.
- **108th pada** — Kalyanraman alone (vol.1 p.126: Aswini 1 → Revati 4, Sravana 4).
- **Vainashika** — three readings: **the 23rd** (Shridhar's "majority"; Narada;
  Govinda's commentary on MC; Kalyanraman vol.2 "23rd Vinaasana"; Jataka
  Parijata IX.79 on `/gochara-vedha`), the 22nd (Kalaprakasika p.167; Wilhelm,
  who exempts ten birth stars), or the star holding the 88th pada (Kalyanraman
  vol.1 p.139, vol.2 p.111) — the 22nd for a 1st-pada birth, the 23rd otherwise.

## Tara bala — from the birth star (added 2026-10-10)

Count from the birth star to the muhurta star (birth star = 1); the tara is
the count's place in a nine (Janma, Sampat, Vipat, Kshema, Pratyak, Sadhana,
Naidhana/Vadha, Mitra, Parama Mitra), and 1–9, 10–18, 19–27 are the three
rounds. Checked on the books' examples: MC p.108 (Shatabhisha → Punarvasu, 11,
Sampat), Raman (Aswini → Sravana 22, Kshema; Rohini → Shatabhisha 21, Vipat
of the 3rd round), Joshi's Mrigashira table.

Eight books, by words: Wilhelm 1374, Shridhar 1142, Raman 656, Kalaprakasika
479, Joshi 344, MC 257, Agarwal 179, Kalyanraman 134. Two choices, both
computed:

| Point | Readings |
|---|---|
| Which taras are bad | **1, 3, 5, 7** — Wilhelm, MC's note, Raman, Agarwal · 3, 5, 7 only — Shridhar (1 and 9 "medium"), Joshi, Kalyanraman |
| The rounds | **by round, a quarter in round 2** — MC Gochara 13 "द्वितीयेंऽशका नादिप्रान्त्यतृतीयका … सर्वे तृतीये" (Sharma's English drops Vadha's "third"), Kalaprakasika, Wilhelm, Raman, Kalyanraman vol.2: round 1 the whole star; round 2 only Vipat's 1st, Pratyak's 4th, Vadha's 3rd quarter; round 3 nothing · by round, a third in round 2 — Shridhar, Joshi (Vipat's first, Pratyak's last, Vadha's middle third; Shridhar gives the quarters as KP's) · the whole star in every round — Raman's and Wilhelm's advice for important acts · in urgency, the first 7, 3, 8, 6 ghatis of Janma, Vipat, Pratyak, Naidhana — Raman, Agarwal (a ghati = 1/60 of the star: our reading) |

The janma group in rounds 2 and 3 (the 10th, 19th) is shown as "caution", not
rejected — Wilhelm and Kalaprakasika weaken it, none reject it outright (our
reading). The donations (MC 13, Joshi, Shridhar) and Wilhelm's exceptions,
which need the muhurta lagna, are shown, not computed.

## Chandra bala — from the natal Moon's sign (added 2026-10-10)

The house of the Moon's sign from the natal Moon's sign, and the paksha. By
words: Wilhelm 345, Joshi 323, Raman 158, MC 143, Rangacharya 139, Shridhar
128, Agarwal 68, Vashistha 47, Kalyanraman 17. Wilhelm writes only on the 8th,
so the house list's default is Joshi's, the first of the books that give one.
Four readings, all computed:

| Reading | Books |
|---|---|
| **good in 1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11 (bright half also 2, 5, 9) with no vedha** — a planet other than Mercury in 5, 9, 12, 2, 4, 8 respectively (bright half 6, 4, 8) | Joshi pp.44–45, MC p.102 (the same six pairs), MC p.30 (without the 1st), Shridhar (bright-half 5th's vedha 12th against Joshi's 4th; his vedha list is misprinted — seven numbers for six houses) |
| not in 6, 8, 12 | Raman, Agarwal |
| not in 4, 8 (12th middling) | Rangacharya, Vashistha ("pujya") |
| not in 4, 8, 12 | Kalyanraman, MC's marriage table p.152 |

**Chandrashtama** (the 8th) is bad in every reading and always shown with
Wilhelm's kind by the tara count: Suddha (14), Sobhana (15), Kaivarta (16),
Amala (17), Siddha (18), and Kshaya in round 3 — Sobhana, Amala and Siddha
harmless ("if Tara is present"). His and Raman's friendship relief is shown
for the person (the natal Moon's lord and the 8th lord; BPHS natural
friendship both ways or one lord — our reading), not applied to the verdict.
Whose chandra bala (the bride's for marriage — Joshi, MC) is left to the user:
the page computes it for the chosen person.

## The ten evils of a marriage muhurta (added 2026-10-11)

Joshi, Vashistha, Agarwal, Shridhar and Kalyanraman (Seegrabodham) list the
same ten: Latta (above), Paata, Yuti, Vedha, Jamitra, Mrityu Panchaka (Bana),
Ekargala, Upagraha, Kranti-samya, Dagdha tithi. Words were measured per evil per
book; the default of each is its most explaining book's (all page images
checked):

| Evil | First by words | Default | Other readings computed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paata | Shridhar 619 | the Moon's star when Harshana, Vaidhriti, Sadhya, Vyatipata, Ganda or Shoola ends — the whole passage (MC VI.60) | only the pada the Moon was in then (Shridhar's 1998-12-09 example); Narada: from the Sun's star to Ashlesha, Magha, Chitra, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati (MC note, Rangacharya, Joshi's Table 6.2, Wilhelm); the same with Swati (Shridhar's text and table); Narada with the Sun's pada number (MC VI.64, Wilhelm) |
| Yuti | Shridhar 478 | any planet in the Moon's sign | not Mercury or Jupiter (MC); a planet in the Moon's star (Joshi, Kalyanraman) |
| Vedha | MC 1014 | Pancha Shalaka (14 pairs; Shridhar's and Rangacharya's list, MC's eight and drawing) | both chakras (Bhoja, Varahamihira); Sapta Shalaka (Agarwal). A malefic strikes the whole star, a benefic only the facing quarter (1-4, 2-3) |
| Jamitra | Shridhar 673 | any planet but Mercury in the 7th sign from the Moon | the 55th navamsa (MC VI.67, "more proper"); a malefic in the 14th star (Vashistha, Agarwal, Rangacharya) |
| Bana | Shridhar 867 | Mrityu bana by the Sun's completed degree (1, 10, 19, 28) | Wilhelm's count (0-1° is the first degree); tithi + lagna (south India) — lagnas listed |
| Ekargala | Kalyanraman 477 (Shridhar 462) | Kharjura chakra with Abhijit (Kalyanraman's third norm = Shridhar's only one) | without Abhijit (Rangacharya, Kalaprakasika); the odd count from the Sun, with and without Abhijit (MC VI.62, Joshi, Agarwal; Narada); Wilhelm's (yoga + 7, eight stars exempt) |
| Upagraha | Shridhar 284 | the 13 counts 5, 7, 8, 10, 14, 15, 18, 19, 21-25 (MC VI.63) | the Sun's pada number only (MC VI.64; M.P., M.G.; Wilhelm); counting Abhijit (Joshi); M.P.'s eight |
| Kranti-samya | Shridhar 590 | the six sign pairs (MC VI.61) | — |
| Dagdha | Shridhar 214 | the burnt tithi of either half (MC VI.66) | the bright half for odd signs, the dark for even (Rangacharya, Vashistha) |

How many may be present (words: Joshi 73, Agarwal 71, Shridhar 60, Vashistha
35): **six of the ten avoided** (Joshi, default); the five serious ones (Yuti,
Vedha, Mrityu Bana, Kranti-samya, Dagdha — Shridhar and Joshi by name,
Vashistha by number) and six avoided (Agarwal, Vashistha); the five alone
(Shridhar); all ten; or shown only (for other events). Agarwal gives the same
numbers 3, 4, 6, 8, 10 but lists Upagraha 8th — recorded.

Readings of ours: a body in Abhijit (MC: Uttarashadha's 4th quarter and the
first fifteenth of Shravana) stands on Abhijit and on its own star for Vedha
(MC's own example strikes Mrigashira from that quarter), and on Abhijit alone
for the counts; the running tithi, not the sunrise tithi; the Moon's dignity
(own, exalted, BPHS friend's sign) and the other reliefs are shown, not
applied; the lagna-dependent rules (Jamitra from the lagna, Bana by tithi and
lagna) list the lagnas to leave and are not judged; Ekargala's day-only rule is
shown (no sunrise computed). Kalyanraman's other two Ekargala norms are not
computed (his two lists differ; "end portions" has no measure).
