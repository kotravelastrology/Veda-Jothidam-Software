# KP house significators (Astro Secrets and Krishnamurti Padhdhati, Part 2)

Source: M.P. Shanmugam, *Astro Secrets and Krishnamurti Padhdhati, Part 2* (corpus C52), reprinting T.R. Raghunath's "New Horizons in
Krishnamurti Padhdhati": printed pp.148-149 (rules i-vi) and pp.237-238 (Method A, the golden rule, Method C).

Built in `src/report/kpHouseSignificators.js`, tested by `test-kp-house-significators.js`, carried on the KP report as
`kp.houseSignificators`.

- Level 1: planets in the star of an occupant of the house (strongest, p.148 i).
- Level 2: the occupants (p.237 Method A 2).
- Level 3: planets in the star of the owner (strong only if the house is empty, p.149 iii).
- Level 4: the owner (strong only if the house is empty and no planet is in the owner's star, p.149 iv).
- Golden rule (p.237): three or more from levels 1 and 2, stop there.

Not computed: conjunction and aspect of a significator (p.149 II-III, Western aspects, no orb given); the cusp's star lord and sub
lord (Horary only, p.238); rules v-vi (planets in the star / sub of a star lord). These are left for the owner's choice of
method. The book gives Method A, B and C; they are not ranked.

Open: Parts 1, 3, 4-1, 5 and 6 and Bosmia's birth-time rectification book are copied into the corpus (C51-C57) and text-extracted;
only Part 2's significator pages have been read.
