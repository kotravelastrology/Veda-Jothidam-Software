# Planets in houses, book by book

Page `/planet-house`; engine `src/report/planetHouse.js`; data `fixtures/planet-house/saravali.json`; test `test-planet-house.js`.

Built from Saravali (Kalyana Varma, R. Santhanam translation), chapter 30 "Effects of Planets in Bhavas", verses 2-85, printed pp.473-534 (PDF page = printed - 361): Sun to Saturn in each of the twelve houses from the lagna, 84 cells. Rahu and Ketu are not in this chapter, so the page shows their house and says the book gives no reading.

- Only the verse itself is summarised in Tamil (our own words); the translator's long notes are not used.
- Page images were read for pp.473, 507, 508, 514, 522, 523; the rest from the text layer, whose verse numbers run 2-85 without gaps (the test checks this).
- Two places where the printed book is faulty are flagged on the page: Venus in the 7th (the English wording repeats the 6th-house verse; the Sanskrit and the notes speak of the wife) and Mars in the 2nd (the translation mixes the commentator's gloss into the verse).
- More books go into `BOOKS` in `planetHouse.js` as one fixture each, never merged. Candidates held: Brihat Jataka (Chidambaram 1905, Sastri), Phaladeepika (Sastri), Hora Sara, Jataka Parijata, BPHS, Chamatkara Chintamani, Bhrigu Sutras.

Brihat Jataka (Varahamihira, P.S. Sastri 2007), chapter 20 "Planets in Houses", verses 1-9, printed pp.152-155 (PDF page = printed - 1; page images read for pp.152-155). The book is terse: Mars, Mercury, Venus and Saturn say "the rest as for the Sun / Jupiter", stored as `sameAs` and resolved on the page with a note. Saturn in houses 2-12 is taken as "like the Sun" from the Sanskrit (anyatra); the English line is garbled ("signs other than 7, 9, 11, 12") and the cell says so.

Bhavesh (house lords) - `fixtures/bhavesh/bphs-24.json`, `src/report/bhaveshHouse.js`, `test-bhavesh-house.js`, shown as a second section of `/planet-house`:
BPHS (Santhanam) chapter 24 "Effects of the Bhava Lords", verses 1-144, printed pp.189-235 (PDF page = printed page): each house lord in each of the twelve houses; verse = (lord - 1) x 12 + house. Verse text summarised in Tamil, notes not used; three of the 144 verses were missing from the text layer's numbering and were found by search (2nd lord in 7th, 11th in 3rd, and others); pages 204 and 229 were read from the scan images. Scorpio's lord is Mars and Aquarius's Saturn, as in the book.

Birth star (Janma nakshatra), the first section of the page: `fixtures/janma-nakshatra/{hora-sara-28,brihat-jataka-16}.json`, `src/report/janmaNakshatra.js`, `test-janma-nakshatra.js`.
- Hora Sara (Prithuyasas), chapter 28 "Qualities of Nakshatras", 27 stars a few sentences each (PDF pp.95-97 of `Jyotish_Hora Sara.pdf`; the file is a typed e-text with no printed page numbers, so the scan page is shown and marked). Also stated in the chapter and kept in the fixture's scope: benefic aspect or company makes the results full; a daughter born in Ardra, Ashlesha, Shatabhisha, Pushya, Moola, Chitra, Jyeshtha or Krittika is to be avoided; a son in his father's star brings his mother's death (and the reverse); daughters are not so affected.
- Brihat Jataka (Varahamihira, B.S. Rao 1986), chapter 16 "Influences of Constellations", stanzas 1-14, printed pp.396-399 (PDF = printed - 6; page images of pp.396-399 read), one line per star; the translator's alternative readings (Swati kripalu/trishalu, Vishakha vachana/artha-patu, Shatabhisha strijita-dhani) are kept in the Tamil line. He adds that the results show in proportion to the Moon's strength.
- Neither book gives quarter-wise (pada) results; the translator points to Nakshatra Chudamani, not held. The page shows the pada the Moon is in, but only the star's reading.

Dasha-lord effects (BPHS chapter 47), a section of the same page: `fixtures/dasha-effects/bphs-47.json`, `src/report/dashaEffects.js`, `test-dasha-effects.js`.
- Source: BPHS (Santhanam) chapter 47 "Effects of Dasas", verses 1-89, printed pp.576-589 (PDF page = printed - 489; pages 577, 582 read from the scan). For each of the nine planets: the placements the book names for good results, those for bad results, timing within the dasha, and the translator's notes on yogakaraka lagnas (marked as notes). Verse text summarised in Tamil.
- The book does not say how many of the named placements must hold, so each section shows which hold in the chart, which do not, and which this page cannot check (strength in vargas, navamsha dignity, friendly/benefic signs, aspects, combustion). No verdict is given.
- Checked: exaltation, own sign, moolatrikona (with degrees), debilitation, houses, kendra/trikona, a benefic or malefic in the same sign, the lord of a house in the same sign, being the lord of a house, waning Moon. Rahu/Ketu use the book's own signs (v.34-39). The current Vimshottari mahadasha is marked.
- Not yet: antardasha effects (chapters 52-60).

Lords-of-houses dasha (BPHS chapter 48, `fixtures/dasha-effects/bphs-48.json`, printed pp.590-594, PDF = printed - 489): the one-line effect of the dasha of the lord of each house (v.2-8), the book's note that both the birth placement and the placement at the start of the dasha count (this page reads the birth chart only), the translator's notes (the wealth/marriage side of the 2nd and 7th lords, 3-6-11 lords in their own signs, the Phaladeepika harsha/sarala/vimala yogas), and fourteen rules (v.9-20) for a planet's dasha being favourable or unfavourable. Thirteen are checked from placements and company at birth; the one that needs aspects (a planet aspected by a kendra or trikona lord) is shown as not checked. Each planet card lists the houses it rules, the effect text, and the rules that hold for it. Rahu and Ketu rule no house; the book's remark that they do well in 3, 6, 11 is kept inside the unfavourable-in-3-6-11 rule's text.
