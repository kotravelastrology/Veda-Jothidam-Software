# Vimshopaka Bala

Page `/vimshopaka`; engine `src/chart/vimshopaka.js`; test `test-vimshopaka.js`.
Source: BPHS (Santhanam) chapter 7 vv.17-27, Vol.1 printed pp.95-99 (PDF page = printed page; pages 95, 97, 98, 99 read from the scan).

Four schemes share twenty points among their vargas (Swaviswa): Shadvarga (6, 2, 4, 5, 2, 1), Saptavarga (5, 2, 3, 2½, 4½, 2, 1), Dasavarga (3 for Rasi, 5 for Shashtiamsa, 1½ for the eight others), Shodasavarga (Rasi 3½, Navamsa 3, Shashtiamsa 4, Shodasamsa 2, Hora/Drekkana/Trimsamsa 1 each, ½ for the other nine). In each varga the planet scores the Varga Viswa of its dignity there (own 20, great friend 18, friend 15, equal 10, enemy 7, great enemy 5); strength = sum of Swaviswa x Varga Viswa / 20, with the relation always the natal compound relationship (as in `saptavargajaBala`). The sage's reading: below 5 no good, 5-10 some, 10-15 mediocre, above 15 wholly favourable.

- The book's worked example (Venus, 9s 4°32'30", Shadvarga) is the test: 5.4 + 0.5 + 3.6 + 4.5 + 1.8 + 1.0 = 16.8, every row as printed (including the Hora, lord Moon, a great enemy).
- A printed discrepancy is recorded, not hidden: the translator's table on p.97 lists the Saptavarga column in a different order from the verse (Navamsa 2½, Dvadasamsa 4½, Trimsamsa 2, Saptamsa 1 against the verse's Saptamsa 2½, Navamsa 4½, Dvadasamsa 2, Trimsamsa 1). The verse's order (the usual one) is used.
- The translator's eight grades (Atipoorna 17.5-20 … Atiheena 0-2.5, p.101) are his own numerical assignment and are shown apart from the sage's reading.
- Rahu and Ketu are not included (the book's example and weights are for the seven planets).
- Moolatrikona is treated as own only where the sign is the planet's own sign (the book speaks of "own" vargas).
