# PL9 multi-sample audit (2026-11-04)

Five Parashara's Light printouts were compared page by page with the 88-page book (`/book`): Arjun (12 May 1983 15:05 Bengalooru, `HC4 Arjun full.pdf`, English, 360-day dasha year),
meera (14 Oct 2019 08:31 Stavanger, `meera_HC2.pdf`, 25 pages, year about 365.2422), Kotravel V (15 Nov 1973 08:15 Erode: `vkotra hc4.pdf` 88 pages Lahiri 08:15:41,
`KP2+ kotravel.pdf` 23 pages Lahiri, `KOTRAVEL_HP1.pdf` 56 pages with the KP ayanamsha) and the aniruddha sample of the earlier work. Each printout is a different PL9 setting
(ayanamsha, dasha year): judge a chart only on its own.

Method: every page of the book is built for each birth and its numbers (dates to within a day) are compared with the PDF's text (the scratchpad scripts `buildall.js`, `compare.py`,
`side.py`, `rows.py`); components were then isolated per chart. Regression tests: `test-shadbala-pl9.js`, `test-pl9-reductions.js`, `test-avakhada-extra.js`, and the updated page tests.

## Open (stated, not hidden)

1. **Shad Bala parts that are PL9's own approximations**: Nata-Unnata, Ayana (declination as if the longitude were sidereal + about 24.4, varying by chart), Chesta of Mars, Mercury,
   Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Drik Bala (Jupiter -93.76 on Arjun). Neither the BPHS nor Raman reproduces them; totals, ranks, Ishta/Kashta and Bhava Bala therefore differ from PL9's.
2. **Navamsha dasha start**: Arjun Sagittarius, the sample Aries, Kotravel Aquarius. The "9th from the lagna" inference fits only the sample; none of 100+ candidate points fits all three.
3. **Rath's years for particular signs on Kotravel** (Narayana, Drig, Kendradi): Scorpio (Mars against Ketu) and Sagittarius (Jupiter debilitated, zero years) differ; second-cycle pages (52, 54, 58, 60)
   follow the Rath/Koch rule where the sample's own pages are inconsistent. Arjun's Drig page ends with Gemini and Pisces repeated where the rule gives Aquarius and Scorpio.
4. **Varshesha (year lord)**: neither the Tajika Bhushana selection nor the "roles" one reproduces PL9's choice for more than about half the years; the lord PL9 chooses is always one of the five office holders.
   Annual-chart Ascendants drift a few minutes of arc from PL9's in the later decades (return time differs by seconds).
5. **Kotravel 1973 lunar months**: PL9 prints Chaitradi Kartika, Kartikadi Ashvina; the calendar (Sewell's rules, amanta Kartika from 26 Oct) gives Margashirsha and Kartika. Meera, Arjun and the sample agree with the rule.
6. **Smaller**: sunset degree (PL9 differs by 10"-31" from the Sun's true motion), upagraha/special-lagna seconds (up to 10"), the Sookshma pages' time of day (30 minutes), Saturn transit dates one day late
   (PL9's are UT dates?), Arjun's KP Sun printed a day early by PL9 (its Fortuna uses the right Sun), sample p.37 last antardashas.

## Not built yet

- KOTRAVEL_HP1's extra pages are built as pages 89-92 of the book from this software's own sourced engines, not PL9's canned prose (`extraPages.js`): Mangala dosha (89), Sadhesati phases and remedies (90), gemstones (91), the running and next Vimshottari mahadasha read against BPHS ch.47-48 (92). HP1's per-antardasha result bullets (pp.22-56) are built as pages 100-108 (`antardashaEffects.js`, fixture `fixtures/dasha-effects/bphs-52-60.json`): one page for each of the nine mahadashas from birth, a table for each of its nine antardashas of what BPHS ch.52-60 (Santhanam, printed pp.627-741) say of that antardasha in that dasha, 421 sections in all, with the book, chapter, verse and printed page on every row. Each placement the book names (the antardasha lord's house from the Lagna and from the dasha lord, exaltation, own sign, kendra, trikona, the houses it rules, company, the Navamsha, the waxing or waning Moon) is marked as holding, not holding or not checked; strength, aspects, combustion, friendly and enemy signs, the Vargas and the yogakaraka are not checked, and no verdict is given. The English print has a few printer's errors where the Sanskrit lines above it are legible (Rahu in the Moon's dasha, `2nd or 12th` for the 2nd and 7th; Mercury-Rahu, `8th` for the upachaya houses; Ketu-Rahu, `6th` for the 8th); the fixture's text says so where it follows them, and pp.630, 634, 647, 675, 676, 713, 717, 724, 731 and 739 were read from the scan, the rest from the English text layer. The scan binds printed pp.632-634 as PDF 144, 145, 143. HP1's per-pratyantardasha bullets are built as pages 109-117 (`fixtures/dasha-effects/bphs-61.json`): the same nine mahadashas, each antardasha with its nine pratyantardashas, their dates and what BPHS ch.61 (printed pp.742-769) says of each. The book gives one effect for each of the 81 pairs of antardasha and pratyantardasha lords, the same in every mahadasha, and says only that the bad ones do not come if the antardasha lord is in a trikona, rules an auspicious house, stands in an auspicious house and a benefic Varga; the page shows where that lord stands and gives no verdict. The sookshma and prana levels are built as pages 118-121 and 122-123 (fixtures `bphs-62.json`, `bphs-63.json`): BPHS ch.62 (printed pp.770-784) gives one effect for each of the 81 pairs of pratyantardasha and sookshma lords and ch.63 (pp.785-798) one for each of the 81 pairs of sookshma and prana lords, the same at every higher level. Pages 118-121 take the thirty-six pratyantardashas from the one running on the day of printing (the ones pages 30-32 list), nine to a page, each with its nine sookshma dashas, their date-times and the book's effect of each; pages 122-123 take eighteen sookshma dashas from the one running, each with its nine prana dashas the same way. The book gives no way to weigh the five levels (ch.63 v.83 says only to judge all five together; the translator's note at p.784 says malefic sookshmas in benefic pratyantaras are unfavourable and the reverse), so the pages carry no verdict. The Tamil is a summary of the English print; pp.775, 787, 789, 791 and 795 were read from the scan, and at ch.63 v.24 (Mars-Mercury) the English print is itself cut off after `marria-`. The Kalachakra dasha's results are built as pages 124-126 (`kalachakraEffects.js`, fixtures `bphs-64.json`, `bphs-65.json`): BPHS ch.65 (printed pp.835-841) gives the effect of the dasha of each of the nine signs in the sequence of each of twelve Amsas (the Navamsha sign of the Moon's pada; the book's Sanskrit title calls the chapter the effects of the Kalachakra Navamsha dashas) and ch.64 (pp.825-833) the effect of an antardasha, named by the lord of its sign, in the dashas of each Amsa. Page 124 lists the twelve mahadashas from birth with the ch.65 effect; pages 125-126 the twelve antardashas of each, six mahadashas a page, with the ch.64 effect (none where the book names no effect for that lord). The book gives both for the Savya chakra only and says of the Apsavya (ch.64 v.56-58) only that the antardashas are to be judged from the lords' benefic or malefic nature and their friendship with the dasha lord, so an Apsavya birth gets that rule and no table. ch.65's nine signs for every Amsa are the Savya sequences kalachakraBphs.js already runs (test-kalachakra-effects.js checks this), which confirms the Amsa reading. The English print numbers the Scorpio and Sagittarius Amsa verses 32-33 and 34-40; the Sanskrit numbers are 32-36 and 37-40, which the fixture uses; the Capricorn Amsa has Saturn twice (the first looks like the Sun's in sense, but the Sanskrit says Saturn both times). The antardasha dates are page 41's (the PL9 sample's twelve equal parts), not ch.64 v.1's unequal table. Pages 825, 830, 831, 835 and 836 were read from the scan. Ch.66 onwards (Ashtakavarga, longevity, character, remedies) is not built from these chapters. Not built: HP1's introductory text pages (pp.9-15, general Vedic text and the nature/character card; no sourced equivalent exists in the library yet).

## Page 7 special points: sources and what was built (2026-11-04)

Built in `src/report/specialPoints.js` and shown on page 7 (`subPlanetsPage.js`, test `test-special-points.js`). Arjun and Kotravel reproduce PL9's printed values in every row (Indu Lagna, Beeja Sphuta to the second, Yogi point, Yogi, Avayogi, Duplicate Yogi, 64th Navamsha, 22nd Dreshkana, Sarpa Dreshkana). The Beeja Sphuta and Yogi point add the planets' longitudes rounded to the second, as PL9 does. Not known from any printout: PL9's percentages for the mixed odd/even cases and its wording for a woman's Kshetra Sphuta; the page prints the book's wording there and PL9's own text only for the two cases seen (a man's odd/odd 100% good, a woman's odd/odd 0% poor).
