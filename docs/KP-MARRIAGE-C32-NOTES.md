## Case: "Is marriage promised? Why is there abnormal delay?" (girl b. 15-06-1939 19:05; query 22-10-1969, 10:25 pm New Delhi)
- printed 106 (PDF 111): Rule: the sub-lord of the 7th cusp is the guide. If the sub-lord signifies the houses of marital affairs (2, 7, 11) marriage will take place. Sukra is the chief governor of marriage (printed 107); reckon it if it signifies the houses judged.
- printed 107 (PDF 112): Sani aspect and delay: if Sani connects with houses 2, 7, 11 (occupies, owns, or is in their constellation), it causes delay at each time-period of marriage; if Sani has no connection with 2, 7, 11 but only aspects them, it portends denial/disappointment. "Generally, those with Sani in the 5th house will have marriage delayed till the end" (printed 107).
- printed 107: Krishnamurti Padhdhati goes beyond the 7th: the 2nd house (kutumba, family) and the 11th house (elder brother or sister) should also be examined. Family houses: 4th mother, 5th children, 7th husband/wife, 9th father, 11th elder sibling; 2nd = family generally.
- printed 110 (PDF 113): "Houses to be referred are 2, 7 and 11": 2 = acquisition/family through marriage; 7 = partnership; 11 = lasting friendship.
- printed 110 (PDF 113): KP method for significators of a house, in order: (1) planet in the star of an occupant of the bhava (strongest); (2) occupant; (3) planet in the constellation ruled by the lord of the bhava; (4) planet owning the bhava; (5) planets conjoined with or aspecting these significators and the house.
- printed 111 (PDF 114): Chart's significators come out as Rahu, Budha, Kethu, Surya, Kuja, Guru, Sani, Sukra. The sub-period of Sani (Sani antara) is then the delay.
- printed 111: Rahu in Thula is conjoined with nothing and acts as Sukra's agent; Kethu conjoins Sani (lord of 2) and is aspected by Kuja. "The 2nd, 11th and 7th houses are aspected by Sani; no other planet aspects any of these houses."
- Verdict in the book: the marriage happened on the date of prediction (printed 102/105 region; see the case-end PDF 101 = printed 76 onwards).
- printed 112 (PDF 115): Transits: Krishnamurti rejects the traditional sign-only Gochara reading (Jupiter in 2/3/7/9/11 from Chandra rasi = "Guru bala" for marriage) and insists on actual longitudinal distances and each transiting planet's own star/sub/cusp position. Note: this is a rule against the sign-count method used in some of our Gochara pages.
