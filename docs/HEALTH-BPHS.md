# Health in BPHS (`/health`)

Source: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, R. Santhanam's translation (C23; PDF page = printed + 10), read on the page images 2026-10-26.
Engine `src/report/healthBphs.js`, test `test-health-bphs.js`, page `app/health`.

## What the book gives (read)
- **Ch.3 v.31, printed p.15.** The Sun to Saturn denote bones, blood, marrow, skin, fat, semen and muscles; if the Sun is afflicted at birth the native is liable to disorders of bones, and so on. The book gives no measure of "afflicted": the page shows each planet's sign, house, dignity and malefic company and decides nothing.
- **Ch.4 v.4-4.5, printed p.27 (sthoola).** The limbs of Kalapurusha by the twelve signs from the ascendant: head, face, arms, heart, stomach, hip, space below navel, privities, thighs, knees, ankles, feet. "For the sookshma (minute) scheme of limbs the 36 decanates are resorted to."
- **First house chapter v.11-15, printed pp.94-96 (sookshma).** Head, eyes, ears, nose, temple, chin, face when the first decanate rises; neck, shoulder, arm, side, heart, stomach, navel for the second; pelvis, anus/penis, testicles, thigh, knee, calf, foot for the third; the risen (visible) half is the left side of the body, the unrisen half the right; the ascendant and the seventh house are each in two halves. The three diagrams were checked cell by cell (`test-health-bphs.js`): house 1 = the first limb (both sides), houses 2-6 the 2nd-6th limbs on the right, houses 12-8 the 2nd-6th on the left, house 7 the 7th limb (both sides). Two printed slips in the second diagram are recorded on the page ("Right Side of Head" where "Neck (Right)" belongs; "Right side of Heart" twice where the 4th house's limb is the side of the trunk). v.15: a malefic in a limb gives ulcers or scars, a benefic a mark such as a mole. v.11: the effects of the ascendant apply to the Moon as well, so the limbs are given from both. The sookshma limbs are the ones this chapter names; the book's "36 decanates" of chapter 4 are not given limb by limb in the translation.
- **Sixth house chapter v.9-28, printed pp.116-120.** Diseases in general (the lords of the 6th and 8th in the ascendant with a planet: Sun tumours and fever, Mars blood-vessel disease and wounds, Mercury bilious disease, Jupiter freedom from disease, Venus disease through women, Saturn windy disease, Rahu danger from low-caste men, Ketu disease of the navel, the Moon water and phlegmatic disorders), the timing of illness (v.13-19.5: seven conditions with ages 6/12, 19/22, 26, 29/30, 45, 59 and illness throughout life), and unfortunate years (v.20-28: animals at 8, fire at 1-2 and birds at 3, water at 5 and 9, small-pox at 10 and 30, blood vessels and urinary disorders at 18 and 22, loss of wealth at 31, inimical sons, dogs at 10 and 19). Each is a condition over the rasi chart; the engine reports HELD, NOT_HELD or UNKNOWN and the ages are the book's.

## Conventions (mine)
Malefic = Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu; for the limb marks, benefic = Jupiter, Venus. "Inimical" is natural enmity. Houses are whole signs. Lords are the sign's single lord (Mars for Scorpio, Saturn for Aquarius). The Moon-as-ascendant reading is not applied to the sixth-house rules. Mandi from `upagraha.js` (UNKNOWN if it cannot be had). Rule v.25 uses the translator's note (the 8th lord with Rahu in the 2nd, 4th, 5th, 11th or 12th from the ascendant and in the 8th from the navamsha ascendant). Rule v.23 follows the translation ("the Moon in the 12th from the said Sun").

## Not built
Rules by "respective significator and bhavas for relatives" (printed p.116 note); Dr. Cornell's lists of diseases by planet quoted by the translator (printed pp.116-118, a modern medical list); PL9's own Health report ("Influence of Grahas on Physiology": Sookshma and Sthoola schemes by planet) whose source is not named on its screen: our page follows BPHS, not PL9's table, and does not reproduce it.
