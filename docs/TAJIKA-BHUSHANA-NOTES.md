# Tajika Bhushana (Ganesha Daivajna), reading notes for the Varshaphala interpretations

Source: *Tajika Bhushana* of Ganesha Daivajna, Sanskrit verses with a Hindi commentary (corpus C28, `C28_Tajika_Bhushana_Ganesha.pdf`). Printed page =
PDF page - 4 (PDF 14 = printed 10). Read on the page images at double size, 2026-10-26. Verse numbers are those the book prints; "TI" is the Hindi
commentary. Everything below is recorded as printed; where the commentary and the verse differ, both are given.

## Chapter 1 (Varshesha-nayana), printed 10: who can be Varshapati (v.34-37)
Pancha-adhikari: the janma-lagna lord, the varsha-lagna lord, the Munthesha, the lord of the day-sign (Sun by day) or of the night-sign (Moon by night)
at the varsha entry, and the Trirashipati. Of these the planet with full strength that also aspects the varsha lagna fully is Varshapati (v.35); if the
aspects are equal the stronger, if the strength is equal the one with more aspect, and if all are equal the one with the most adhikara; with equality of all,
the Munthesha (v.36). If no planet aspects, the one that aspects the janma lagna or the sign of the lagna is taken; failing that the Munthesha (v.37).
(Compare `varsheshaSelection` in `src/report/varshaphala.js`.)

## Chapter 2 (Muntha-phala), printed 10-16
v.1 (printed 10-11): the Munthesha at the varsha entry in the 8th, 2nd, 6th or 12th is not praised (the commentary on printed 10 lists 2, 6, 12 and the first
word before them is overwritten), or when it is joined to or aspected by the lord of the 8th, or by sinful planets, or is combust or retrograde.
v.2: the Munthesha in the 9th, 11th, 3rd or in a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) increases a man's fame, gives good counsel and the king's favour.
v.3: the Munthesha, or its friend, a strong benefic joined to the Muntha or aspecting it, fulfils all wishes and brings honour from the king.
v.4: Rahu-mukha and puccha: counting from the nakshatra Rahu occupies, the nakshatras still to be crossed are Rahu's face and those already crossed its tail;
Muntha in the face gives honour and fortune, in the tail sorrow.

Muntha with a planet (printed 11-13):
- v.5 Sun: wealth and honour through the king, gain of Lakshmi, increase of honour, destruction of enemies.
- v.6 Moon: bodily comfort, meeting good people, good sense, wealth, contentment, honour from the king.
- v.7 Mars: loss of good sense and growth of bad, bodily illness from blood disorder, loss of wealth, trouble over home and food, dislike among kin.
- v.8 Mercury: gain of good people, hearing of stories, cheerfulness, love among kin, wealth by honest means.
- v.9 Jupiter: pleasures with women, children, growth of good sense, honour, meeting good people, all-round increase of wealth. The commentary adds: "here the
  result of Venus too".
- v.10 Saturn (the verse runs off the edge of the printed page and the commentary has no entry on printed 14-15 for it): not read.

Muntha in the twelve bhavas (printed 13-15; v.15's commentary prints "9" for the fifth, which a reader has corrected to 5):
- v.11 in the varsha lagna: good company, honour from the king, gain, skill, standing among people, relief of kin's distress.
- v.12 in the 2nd: wife's happiness, fame, loss of what is unwanted, trouble to enemies, accumulation of wealth.
- v.13 in the 3rd: growth of fame, fulfilled wishes, fixed mind in right conduct, standing in society.
- v.14 in the 4th: sorrow from daring in earning money, discord in the family, fear from the king, loss of right conduct, loss of standing.
- v.15 in the 5th: good deeds and bodily purity, enemies' disregard, relief of mental pain, a place among kings.
- v.16 in the 6th: enmity throughout the kin, illness, fear of enemies, fear from the king even when serving.
- v.17 in the 7th: separation from family and from the wife, loss of wealth, fame and right conduct, grief and illness.
- v.18 in the 8th: various troubles, quarrels, loss of wealth, growth of illness, an unsettled mind, a very inauspicious year.
- v.19 in the 9th: honour from the king, growth of fame, benefit to kin, increase of merit.
- v.20 in the 10th: fulfilment of one's wishes, prosperity, increase of Lakshmi, mind on good works.
- v.21 in the 11th: growth of understanding like Jupiter's, love of sons and kin.
- v.22 in the 12th: the door of gaining wealth closed, bad counsel, trouble from sons and kin, mind turned from worship.
- v.23: if Muntha is in a good place from the varsha lagna and in a bad place from the janma lagna (or the reverse) the result is mixed, more or less by the Muntha's strength.
- v.24: all these results (good, mixed, bad) are to be raised or lowered by the strength of the Munthesha.
Printed 15 ends the chapter ("iti Muntha-phaladhyaya 2").

## Chapter 3 (Varshesha-phala), printed 16 on
v.1: the commentary says the Varshapati's selection is already given; the chapter gives results as the ancients taught, good, middling and bad.
v.2: the varsha-lagna lord, Varshapati, Muntha and Munthesha, by their strength, give the year's results, each in its own dasa.
v.3: a Varshapati in the 8th, 6th or 12th, or combust, joined to or aspected by sinful planets, is not good; strong, a benefic, or in its own place it gives its dasa good wealth.
v.4: the Sun as strong Varshapati: gain through the king by ancestry, many kinds of bodily comfort, wealth, destruction of enemies.
(continued on printed 17-21 for the other planets.)

### Chapter 3 continued: results of each planet as Varshapati by its strength (printed 17-21)
For each planet the book gives three verses: the planet strong (purna-bala), of middle strength (madhya-virya) and weak (kshina or hina-virya). It gives no numeric
threshold for the three grades in these verses.
- Sun: strong v.4 (printed 16); middle v.5: a little happiness from the king, son and friends, quarrels in words, obstacles, fear, mutual opposition; weak v.6:
  going abroad, growth of enemies, loss of sense, repeated loss of wealth, fights with bad people, blame, illness.
- Moon: strong v.7: good gain from the king, many comforts, pleasure with women, gain of white cloth and pearls; middle v.8: some less happiness from the wife, bodily
  unsteadiness, loss of wealth among kin, weakness from the king's displeasure; weak v.9: wind-borne pain in the body, quarrels in the family, travel abroad, fear
  of the king, trouble of phlegm.
- Mars: strong v.10: victory over enemies, gain of good things through the king or a commander on travels; middle v.11: bodily illness, mutual disputes, enmity,
  loss of wealth, fear of thieves, enemies and the king; weak v.12: trouble from both friends and enemies, illness of foot, mouth and eyes, harm from bad people, the king and fire.
- Mercury: strong v.13: gain of wealth, pleasure with women, many undertakings, knowledge of astrology and medicine; middle v.14: ill luck from one's own speech, anger
  between wife and husband, grief from enemies, more favour of the king; weak v.15: false witness, fear from the king, loss of wealth by enemies and thieves,
  illness of the eyes, hands and throat.
- Jupiter: strong v.16 (printed 19): growth of knowledge, fame, a store of wealth, respect from kin, worship, a good name, devotion, wealth from the royal family, renown;
  middle v.17: quarrels with people, dullness of mind, growth of enemies, fear of the king, weakness; weak v.18: wind pain in the thigh, eyes, ankle, foot and belly, fear of the wicked.
- Venus: strong v.19 (printed 20): pleasures with women, laughter, festivals, gain of cloth, health, growth of fortune and victory and of kin; middle v.20: fear from kin and
  enemies, worry about sons, daughters, wife and wealth, weakness from increase of phlegm; weak v.21: much quarrel with bad people, bewilderment from the king's wrath, fear, loss of wealth, wrong judgment.
- Saturn: strong v.22: gain of the wished-for through hills, forests, a prostitute's trade, farming and foreigners, liking for gardens; middle v.23: pain in chest, back, neck and eyes,
  great fear of foreigners, weakness; weak v.24 (printed 21): loss of strength and lustre through wind, failure of one's own purposes, grief for kin.
Printed 21 ends chapter 3 and begins chapter 4, the four kinds of Harsha-sthana (Sun in the 9th, Moon in the 3rd, Mars in the 6th, Mercury in the 1st, Jupiter in the 11th, Venus in the
5th, Saturn in the 12th; by day the male planets, by night the female ones; the male planets in 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12 and the female in 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 from the lagna).

## Chapters 7-9: the mixed chapter, the Mudda dasha and its results, printed 71-90
Read 2026-10-26 on the page images (PDF 73-94 at double size; the dasha chapter's own table read at four times).

**Chapter 8, Dasha-sadhana (printed 73-76).** v.1: planets' results are given; they cannot be timed without the dasha order, so the method follows.
v.2: take the varsha lagna and the seven planets, leave the sign, and arrange them by their degrees-and-minutes within the sign, the smallest first; each is
built up by the preceding ones ("hinamsha"), and the differences between neighbours are the "patyamsha". v.3: if two have equal degrees put the stronger
first; if also equal, the one with the smaller enjoyment first. v.4: the sum of the patyamsha equals the largest hinamsha; take the solar year as 360 days,
multiply by 60 twice (1,296,000) and divide by the patyamsha total in vikalas, which gives the dhruvanka; each patyamsha by the dhruvanka (gomutrika
multiplication) gives that planet's dasha in days, ghatis and palas. v.5: the antardasha is made the same way inside each dasha.
The book's worked table (printed 74), planets in the order Mercury, Lagna, Sun, Venus, Jupiter, Mars, Saturn, Moon:
hinamsha 2d18'34", 8d53'2", 9d44'59", 16d26'26", 19d23'32", 19d57'8", 21d57'3", 28d16'31"; patyamsha 2d18'34", 6d34'28", 0d51'57", 6d41'27", 2d57'6",
0d33'36", 1d59'55", 6d19'26"; dasha 29-22-6, 83-44-28, 9-19-34, 85-42-17, 33-46-43, 7-6-44, 25-26-46, 80-31-22 (days-ghatis-palas). The patyamsha sum is

= 29.4 days (printed 29-22-6 = 29.37), Lagna 83.7 (83-44-28), Moon 80.5 (80-31-22), Venus 85.2 (printed 85-42-17 = 85.7), but Sun 11.0 days (printed
9-19-34) and Jupiter 37.6 (printed 33-46-43) do not follow from their patyamsha; the printed days add to 355, not 360. The table is recorded as printed.
The app's `varshaphala.js` Patyayini dasha (after Neelakantha) uses a 365.25-day year; the book's year is 360 days. Same method otherwise.
v.6-9 (printed 76): "Prashna-upari varsha-nayana", finding the year's chart from a question when the birth time is unknown: the Muntha is the number
got by dividing the degrees-minutes-seconds of the question lagna (sign left out) by 150, counted from Aries; the entry is when the next year's Sun equals the
Sun at the question.

**Chapter 8 continued, results of each planet's dasha (printed 76-83).** Five verses per planet: strong (purna-bala), middle (madhya), weak (hina),
destroyed (nashta-bala), then a rule that a planet in good places gives better. The book gives no threshold for the four grades. Sun v.1-5 (printed 76-77),
Moon v.6-10 (77-78), Mars v.11-15 (78-79), Mercury v.16-20 (79-80), Jupiter v.21-25 (80-81), Venus v.26-30 (81-82), Saturn v.31-35 (82-83). The rules:
Sun in 3, 6, 11 with lost strength gives the weak result stated, middle strength the middle one, strong the larger (v.5); Moon in 2, 3, 11 with lost
strength does not give the best (v.10); Mars in 11, 6, 3 gives good even with lost strength (v.15); Mercury with lost strength not in 8, 12, 6 gives the best
in its dasha (v.20); Jupiter not in 12, 6, 8 does not give evil even if weak (v.25); Venus condemned but not in 12, 6, 8 does not trouble much (v.30);
Saturn in 11, 3 or 6 does not give the whole evil (v.35). v.36: the lagna's dasha by drekkana: movable lagna 1st best, 2nd middle, 3rd hard; fixed 1st
inauspicious, 2nd auspicious, 3rd middle; dual 1st middle, 2nd inauspicious, 3rd auspicious. v.37: the lagna's results follow the planets' graded results
by the lagna lord. "Iti dasha-phaladhyaya" at printed 83.

**Chapter 9, Antar-dasha-phala (printed 83-90), 43 verses.** The first verse is a preface; v.2-43 give the result of each antardasha lord within each
dasha lord, six per planet, 42 in all, in the book's order: Sun's dasha (Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn), Moon's (Sun, Mars, Mercury,
Jupiter, Venus, Saturn), Mars' (Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Sun, Moon), Mercury's (Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Sun, Moon, Mars), Jupiter's (Venus, Saturn, Sun,
Moon, Mars, Mercury), Venus' (Saturn, Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter), Saturn's (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus). The Rahu and Ketu are not
given a dasha. The order of the antardashas within a dasha in time is not stated in the verses.
Misprint: v.36 (Venus' dasha, antardasha of the Moon's son = Mercury) the commentary prints "mangala" (Mars), with a hand correction above it in this copy;
the verse's word "shashankaja" is Mercury, and Mars has its own v.35. Chapter 10 (printed 90 on, the monthly entry) not read.

## The sahams, printed 68-71 (chapter "atha sahamani", 11 verses)
Read 2026-10-26 on the page images (PDF 72-75, at three times size for the verse and commentary lines). The book gives each saham as "from X take Y and add
the lagna" ("A ko B mein ghataye"; the commentary's phrase "X ko Y mein ghataye" means subtract X from Y). By day or night: where a formula is "DAY" the
book says "at night the reverse"; where it is "BOTH" it is the same by night and day.
v.1 (printed 68): punya = Moon - Sun + lagna (day); rajya = Saturn - Sun + lagna (day). v.2 (69): jiva = Saturn - Jupiter + the janma lagna (day); mrityu = the 8th
bhava - Moon + Saturn (both). v.3: matri = Moon - Venus + lagna (day); bhratri = Jupiter - Saturn + lagna (both). v.4: pitri = Sun - Saturn + lagna (both); putra =
Jupiter - Moon + lagna (both). v.5: yasha = punya - Jupiter + lagna (day); bala = Mars - punya + lagna (day). v.6 (69-70): vidya = Sun - Moon + lagna (day); gaurava =
Jupiter - Moon + Sun (day). v.7 (70): karma = Mars - Mercury + lagna (day); paradesha = the lord of the punya saham minus the punya saham, "sanga" (the verse) = with
the lagna, which the commentary does not say (both). v.8: dhana = the lord of the 2nd - the 2nd bhava + lagna (both); labha = lagna - the lagna lord + lagna (both).
v.9: karyasiddhi: by day Sun - Saturn + the lord of the Sun's sign; the night wording ("the Moon taken out and the Sun's sign lord added") does not say from what, so it is
left unclear. v.10: stri (kalatra) = Venus - the lord of the 7th + the 7th bhava (both); vyapara = Mercury - Mars + lagna (both). v.11 (71): a saham joined or aspected by
strong benefics or its own lord grows; if its lord makes ithasala with the Varshapati its fruit is certain; a saham that is lost (combust), weak, or with malefics is
harmed. "Iti sahamadhyaya" at printed 71; the mixed chapter ("mishraka", printed 71-73) follows and is not read.
The 30-degree correction that the app's `sahams.js` applies is not in these pages. Compared with the app's table on 5 Oct 1990 (age 36): punya, matri and karma agree;
putra, vidya and gaurava differ by exactly the 30 degrees; the others differ in formula (rajya: the app has Sun - Jupiter; mrityu: the app adds the lagna, the book Saturn;
bhratri: the app adds the lagna lord, the book the lagna; pitri: the app has Saturn - Sun, the book Sun - Saturn; bala: the app has punya - Mars; dhana: the app has the 2nd bhava minus its
lord; jiva, labha, paradesha, karyasiddhi, vyapara also differ: 11 in all). Yasha and stri have no counterpart in the app.

## The last chapter: the monthly entry, the daily entry, food (printed 90-96, 27 verses)
Read 2026-10-26 on the page images (PDF 94-100 at 2.2 times). It follows "iti Tajikabhushane dashantardashaphalaadhyayah" (printed 90).
v.1: the results of the monthly entry are very subtle; the method of casting it is given for the ganakas (computers). v.2: put the birth Sun in 11 places and add 1, 2,
3 ... signs in turn; the first monthly entry's Sun is the varsha entry's (example: birth Sun 7-24-59-19 gives 8-24-59-19 for the second month, 9-24-59-19 for the
third, and so on). v.3: the monthly entry's mean Sun in vikalas, times 13 ("gunabhu") divided by 890 ("khananda-naga"), added to the same vikalas, gives the ahargana with its
parts (an almanac computation). v.4: the lagna for the entry's Sun is the month's lagna; the Moon and the other planets are placed in their own places. v.5: the lord of the
navamsha of the lagna lord and the lord of the navamsha of the lagna: whichever is stronger is the month lord. v.6-12: results by the month lord (Sun v.6, Moon v.7, Mars v.8,
Mercury v.9, Jupiter v.10, Venus v.11, Saturn v.12). v.13: these are for strong lords; for weak, middle and lowest strength infer by analogy. v.14: the daily results follow the dasha
division as in the year. "Iti masaphalani" at printed 93.
Daily entry (dinacharya, v.15-20, printed 93-94): adding one degree to the entry Sun for each day gives the next day's Sun; the day's lagna and planets by that Sun (v.16). The lagna
lord in the 7th from the lagna or the Moon: great happiness; Mercury, Venus or Jupiter in the 11th, 3rd or 6th: victory, even a great work done (v.17); the lagna lord in the 6th or 8th with
Jupiter weak or with malefics: many diseases (v.18); a malefic in the 8th or in an enemy's sign: fear of enemies, worry, dejection, quarrels that day (v.19); benefics in kendra, trikona and the 2nd
and malefics in the 3rd, 11th, 6th: victory, pleasure, wealth that day (v.20). "Iti dinacharya" at printed 94.
Food (bhojana-vichara, v.21-24, printed 94-95): the lagna lord gives the food, the 4th lord is the thing eaten, the lord of the 7th is hunger, the 10th lord the eater; the lagna lord and the
11th lord joined or aspected by benefics: excellent food (v.21). Venus and Jupiter in the lagna: good food; Rahu, Sun, Saturn there: harm; malefics joined or aspected by benefics: food with trouble
(v.22). Sun strong: food with sesame; Moon: rice; Mars: gram; Mercury: mung dal; Jupiter: wheat and kangni; Venus: bajra and gram; Saturn: urad (v.23). Tastes: Sun pungent, Moon salty, Mars sharp,
Mercury mixed, Jupiter sweet, Venus sour, Saturn astringent (v.24). "Iti bhojanavicharah" at printed 95.
Colophon (v.25-27, printed 95-96): the Tajika Bhushana is an ornament only for those who value the virtues of others; Ganesha Daivajna, son of Dhundhiraja, of Parthapura on the Godavari; the
Hindi translation by Pandit Sitaram Shastri, Lucknow, finished Samvat 1992 Phalguna Krishna 14, Wednesday, at the instance of Khemraj Shrikrishnadas, Shri Venkateshvar Steam Press, Bombay.

to Saturn in the order of the verse (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn: Aries, Taurus, Capricorn, Virgo, Cancer, Pisces, Libra); the 7th from each is the fall. v.24-29 (printed 7): the exaltation degrees (Sun 10,
Moon 3, Mars 28, Mercury 15, Jupiter 5, Venus 27, Saturn 20) and, for each sign, the hadda divisions (in the table on printed 8: five bands per sign, planet and degrees; transcribed in `src/report/panchaVargeeyaBook.js`: the verses give the same degrees in number-words, which settle the digits that do not total 30 in the table: Gemini Venus 6 not 8, Scorpio Jupiter 5 not 7, Pisces Mars 9 not 6; Virgo is Mercury 7, Venus 6, Jupiter 4, Mars 7, Saturn 6 in table and verse, unlike the Egyptian terms). v.30 (printed 8): the drekkana lords by sign: the first, second and third drekkana
take the planets in the order Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Sun, Moon cycled along the 36 drekkanas from Aries (the table on printed 8 shows this: 10 degrees: Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Sun, Moon, Mars,
Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn; 20: Sun, Moon, Mars ...); counted along the first drekkanas of Aries to Pisces, then the second, then the third (the table gives Mars, Sun, Venus for Aries; the verse says "from Mars, the Sun, Venus", the commentary wrongly the Moon for the second). v.31 (printed 9): the mushallaha lords: the table gives three lords for each sign, the same for the four signs of each triplicity (Aries Sun, Jupiter, Saturn; Taurus Venus, Moon, Mars; Gemini Saturn, Mercury, Jupiter; Cancer Venus, Mars, Moon), read as the first, second and third ten degrees. v.32:
the strength of own house, exaltation, hadda, drekkana and mushallaha is 30, 20, 15, 10 and 5 (the commentary prints "30, 20, 19, 10, 9", a misprint); v.33 and the table on printed 9: a planet in a friend's place gets
three quarters of that figure (22.5, 11.25, 7.5, 3.75 for own house, hadda, drekkana and mushallaha), in a neutral's place a half (15, 7.5, 5, 2.5), in an enemy's a quarter (7.5, 3.75, 2.5, 1.25). `panchaVargeeyaBook.js` follows these (maximum 80); the app's older `panchaVargeeyaBala` (Rao) gives a friend half, uses the Parashari drekkana and the Egyptian terms and divides by 4, and stays as `strengthMethod: 'APP'`. On the audit chart the Varshapati differs between the two in 4 of the first 60 years. Exaltation is 20 in the exaltation sign and nothing elsewhere (no graded scale in the verses); friendship is the natural one (the chapter gives no list).
v.34-37 (printed 10): the selection, as in `src/report/varsheshaBook.js`. The commentary on v.34 says "lagna lord of the Sun" by day; the verse says "arkesha", the lord of the Sun.
Printed 10 closes chapter 1 ("iti varshesha-nayanadhyaya 1") and begins chapter 2, already read.
